What has been reviewed

California Youth Connection members reviewed the app in September 2026. Their feedback led to changes in readability, navigation, and Spanish content. This does not imply CYC endorsement or a formal partnership.

Program explanations link to their sources. Staff should check the guidance before recommending it. Young people should confirm eligibility, dollar amounts, and deadlines with their caseworker or ILP coordinator.

The app stores profile answers on the user’s device. It does not send those answers to Avery. There is no central usage counter. English and Spanish are available. Vietnamese and Chinese remain drafts awaiting native-speaker review.

Three easy ways to try it

  1. Staff review. Open the sample timeline and flag unclear or outdated information.
  2. Small youth trial. Offer the tool to a few young people during an existing session. Participation and feedback are optional.
  3. Share after review. Add the link to a resource sheet, newsletter, workshop, or online resource list if it is suitable.
