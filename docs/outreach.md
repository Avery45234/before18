# Getting an expert review and real users

Two things make the submission credible: one person who does this work saying the
rules are right, and two or three young people or caseworkers who tried it.

## Who to write to (California)

- Your county ILP coordinator (list: https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/foster-care/county-contacts-for-efc-thp-and-ilp)
- John Burton Advocates for Youth - https://jbay.org (they publish the THP-Plus and extended foster care guides the app cites)
- California Youth Connection - https://calyouthconn.org (youth-led; chapters statewide)
- Your county CASA program - https://www.californiacasa.org
- Your school district's foster youth liaison (the front office knows who it is)
- The California Foster Care Ombudsperson's office - https://fosteryouthhelp.ca.gov

## The message (edit it into your own words)

Subject: A student-built tool for youth aging out - could you check it for accuracy?

Hi [name],

I'm Avery, a high school student in [city]. For the Congressional App Challenge I
built a free web app called Before 18 for foster youth in California who are about
to turn 18. You enter a birthdate and a few yes/no questions, and it shows what you
are eligible for and when it opens and closes: extended foster care, Medi-Cal to 26,
the Chafee Grant, THP-Plus, the exit documents federal law requires, and so on. It
also shows what a decision would cost - for example, closing a case a few days
before the 18th birthday.

Every rule cites its official source (CDSS, DHCS, CSAC, federal statute) and the date
I checked it. But I am not a caseworker, and real young people would act on this,
so I would be grateful if someone who does this work could look at the eligibility
rules and tell me what is wrong or missing. It takes about ten minutes:

[link] - the rules are on the "Timeline" screen; "About" lists every source.

If it is useful, I would also love to hear whether a youth or a caseworker would
actually use it, and I am happy to change anything.

Thank you for the work you do.

Avery [last name]
[school], [email]

## What to ask a tester

1. Did you understand what to do in the first 30 seconds?
2. Was anything wrong, or different from what your caseworker told you?
3. Did the "What if" screen change how you think about the 18th birthday?
4. Would you send this to someone? Who?

Write down exactly what they said. One honest sentence from a real user is worth a
minute of your demo video.
