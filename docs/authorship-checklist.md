# Making the code yours (authorship checklist)

The Congressional App Challenge's AI policy, in three rules (see
https://elfreth.house.gov/services/congressional-app-challenge and the official site,
https://www.congressionalappchallenge.us/):

1. **Full disclosure.** Say completely how and where AI was used, in the submission
   materials. That is `AI_ASSISTANCE_LOG.md`, the "AI use and authorship" section of
   the About page, and the AI question on the submission form. All three must agree.
2. **Supportive role only.** AI cannot be the entirety of the technical creation.
3. **Significant personal contribution** and a clear technical understanding of how
   the app works. Judges may ask for the source and ask you to explain it.
